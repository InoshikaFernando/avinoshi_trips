Fiji Layover — Exploring Nadi

A tropical bonus between Japan and home
🇫🇯 Fiji Transit Thu 6 Feb 2025
The return Fiji layover was long enough to step out of the airport and explore — and as a family that loves covering as many countries as possible, we weren't going to waste it. We got out into Nadi, soaked up a bit of tropical Fiji, and gave the kids one last adventure before the short final flight home to Auckland. After a week of Japanese winter, a few hours in tropical Fiji felt like a bonus holiday.
🌴 Exploring Nadi
With a 10+ hour layover on the return leg, we took the chance to step out of Nadi Airport and explore. It's a great way to break the journey and add another country to the trip. Nadi town is close to the airport, and Denarau beach or a resort day pass are easy options. The kids loved the change of scenery — from cold Japanese winter to warm tropical Fiji in a single flight.
🇫🇯 Nadi, Fiji ⏱ 10+ hour layover 🌴 Time to explore
💡 Guide Tips — Fiji Transit Layover
  • Leaving the airport: With a 10+ hour layover, you have plenty of time to exit the airport and explore Nadi. Keep your boarding pass and passport handy for re-entry
  • Nadi town: Just a short taxi ride from the airport. Markets, local food, and a relaxed tropical vibe
  • Denarau: A resort area about 20 minutes from the airport. Some resorts offer day passes for pools and beach access
  • Currency: Fijian Dollar (FJD). ATMs available at the airport. Many places accept card
  • With kids: The change of pace from busy Japan to relaxed Fiji is lovely. Let them splash around and unwind before the final flight
🍽 Lunch in Nadi
Fijian Lunch
After a week of Japanese cuisine, a Fijian lunch was a refreshing change. Nadi has plenty of casual restaurants and cafes near the town centre — a tropical meal in warm sunshine was the perfect way to break the long journey home.

Morning: Arrive Nadi, Fiji from overnight flight
Daytime: Explore Nadi, lunch in town
