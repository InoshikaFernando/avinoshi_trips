Universal Studios — Day 1

The Wizarding World of Harry Potter
🇯🇵 Japan· Osaka Osaka Thu 30 Jan 2025
Universal Studios Japan in Osaka is a must-visit for families, and we'd strongly recommend buying a 2-day Studio Pass. With young kids, one day just isn't enough — you'll be managing nap times, snack breaks, and toilet runs, and rushing through the park takes the joy out of it. Two days let you soak it all in at a relaxed pace. Day 1 was all about The Wizarding World of Harry Potter.
🏰 The Wizarding World of Harry Potter
Walking through the gates into Hogsmeade Village is genuinely magical — the snow-covered roofs, the crooked chimneys, and Hogwarts Castle looming above it all. The attention to detail is extraordinary. The main attraction is Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey inside the castle, a motion-based dark ride that takes you flying through the Hogwarts grounds. Flight of the Hippogriff is a family-friendly outdoor coaster perfect for younger kids. Don't skip the wand experience at Ollivanders, and grab a butterbeer (the frozen version is amazing even in winter).
📍 USJ, Osaka 🎟 2-day Studio Pass 👶 All ages — some rides have height limits
💡 Guide Tips — Universal Studios Japan
  • 2-day pass is worth it: Especially with kids under 5. You avoid the pressure of cramming everything into one exhausting day
  • Get there early: Gates often open 15–30 minutes before the official time. Being in the first wave in means shorter queues for the first hour
  • Harry Potter area entry: Sometimes requires a timed entry ticket on busy days. Check the USJ app on the morning of your visit
  • Express Pass: Consider buying Universal Express Pass if it's a busy period — it lets you skip queues on select rides
  • Height limits: Forbidden Journey requires 122 cm. Flight of the Hippogriff requires 92 cm. Check your kids' heights before promising rides!
  • Stroller parking: Stroller parks are available at every major ride entrance. Staff are incredibly helpful
  • Food inside the park: Expensive but fun — themed meals in the Three Broomsticks restaurant are part of the experience
⚑ Day 1 Highlights
  • Hogwarts Castle — jaw-dropping from every angle
  • Harry Potter and the Forbidden Journey ride
  • Flight of the Hippogriff — perfect for younger kids
  • Hogsmeade Village — Ollivanders, Honeydukes, butterbeer
  • Relaxed pace with a 2-day pass — no rushing
