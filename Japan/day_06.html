Sunday Mass & Back to Tokyo

Faith, farewell to Kyoto, and the Shinkansen east
🇯🇵 Japan· Kyoto → Tokyo Travel Day Sun 2 Feb 2025
Even on the road, Sunday mass matters to our family. Japan has a small but vibrant Catholic community — Catholicism arrived with Portuguese missionaries in the 16th century — and Kyoto has several churches with English-friendly services. We attended morning mass, then packed up and caught the Shinkansen back to Tokyo, checking into our Disney area hotel with shuttle access to Tokyo DisneySea.
⛪ Sunday Mass in Kyoto
Attending mass in Japan is a unique experience. The congregations are small but welcoming, and there's something moving about sharing the universal liturgy in a completely different cultural context. Kyoto has several Catholic churches — the Kawaramachi Church (St. Francis Xavier Church) in central Kyoto is one of the most accessible, with services in Japanese but a familiar structure that any Catholic will follow. Some churches offer English masses or bilingual bulletins.
⛪ Catholic Church, Kyoto 🕐 Morning mass
💡 Guide Tips — Catholic Mass in Japan
  • Finding churches: Search "Catholic church" + city name on Google Maps. The Catholic Bishops' Conference of Japan website lists all parishes
  • Mass times: Sunday morning masses are typically at 9:00 or 10:00 AM. Confirm times on the parish website or call ahead
  • Language: Most masses are in Japanese, but the liturgy structure is identical worldwide — you'll know exactly where you are in the service. Some parishes in Tokyo and Osaka offer English masses
  • Etiquette: Japanese congregations are quiet and reverent. Dress modestly. Children are warmly welcomed
  • History: Japan's Catholic community (the Kakure Kirishitan — "hidden Christians") survived 250 years of persecution. The faith runs deep here
📋 Day Schedule
Morning: Sunday mass at a Catholic church in Kyoto
Late morning: Check out of Kyoto hotel, walk to Kyoto Station
Afternoon: Shinkansen — Kyoto → Tokyo (~2 hrs 15 min)
Evening: Check into Disney partner hotel. Ramen dinner — ran into one of Avi's friends!
💡 Guide Tips — Disney Partner Hotels
  • Why a partner hotel? Official Disney hotels are expensive. Partner hotels near Maihama/Shin-Urayasu are much cheaper and run free shuttle buses to the Disney Resort
  • Shuttle frequency: Check the hotel's shuttle timetable before booking — some run every 15 minutes, others hourly
  • Book early: Partner hotels near Disney fill up fast, especially around weekends and holidays
  • Alternative: JR Keiyo Line runs directly to Maihama Station (Disney Resort) from Tokyo Station in about 15 minutes
🍜 Food Highlight — Ramen
Ramen
After arriving in Tokyo, we had ramen for dinner — the ultimate comfort food after a day of travelling. A steaming bowl of rich broth, springy noodles, and tender chashu pork is exactly what you need after hours on the train. The best part? We bumped into one of Avi's friends there — a small-world moment that made the evening extra special. Japan really does feel like it's full of happy surprises.
