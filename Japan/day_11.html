Home to New Zealand

Tadaima! We're home
🇳🇿 New Zealand Home Fri 7 Feb 2025
Home sweet home. After an incredible 11 days — three cities, three theme parks, ancient temples by rickshaw, bullet trains, immersive digital art, and some of the best food we've ever eaten — we landed back in Auckland. Japan with three young kids was ambitious, but absolutely worth it. The combination of smooth transport, family-friendly food, and world-class attractions made it one of our best family trips ever.
🌟 Trip Summary & Verdict
An 11-day Japan winter trip with 2 adults and 3 kids (ages 10, 7, and 3). We covered three cities, three theme parks, ancient temples, bullet trains, and some of the best food on earth — all in winter, which turned out to be an excellent time to visit. Here's what we'd tell other families planning the same trip:
1
The route works
Tokyo → Osaka → Kyoto → Tokyo is a natural loop. The Shinkansen connects everything smoothly. Start and finish in Tokyo to avoid backtracking.
2
Winter is underrated
January/February is cold (5–10°C) but manageable with layers. Fewer tourists at temples and cultural sites, comfortable walking weather, and beautiful misty Kyoto mornings. Theme parks are busy regardless.
3
2-day Universal pass is a must
With kids, one day isn't enough. Two days let you enjoy Harry Potter thoroughly without rushing or stressing about queues.
4
DisneySea needs a strategy
Our biggest regret: missing the Frozen ride. Buy Premier Access for Fantasy Springs rides. Arrive at rope drop. Don't assume winter means short queues.
5
Rickshaw in Kyoto with kids
Best decision of the trip. A rickshaw turns a potentially exhausting temple-walking day into a comfortable, scenic, educational experience. Essential with a toddler.
6
Hand luggage only — game changer
We took just 5 carry-on bags for a family of 5 for 11 days. Packed 3 day kits and 2 night kits, rotated them, and used coin laundry machines every 2–3 days. No checked bags meant no carousel waits, effortless Shinkansen transfers, and total freedom of movement between cities. Japan's coin laundries are everywhere and cheap. This is the single best packing tip we can give.
7
Eat the food
Sushi in Osaka, shabu-shabu in Kyoto, sukiyaki in Tokyo. Japanese food is incredibly kid-friendly — the interactive hot pot meals are a highlight, and even picky eaters can find rice, noodles, and tempura everywhere.
📋 What We'd Do Differently Next Time
  • Buy Premier Access for DisneySea Fantasy Springs rides — non-negotiable
  • Spend an extra day in Kyoto — one day wasn't enough for this incredible city
  • Visit teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills) now that Planets has closed
  • Try Disneyland as well as DisneySea — consider a 2-day Disney pass
  • Explore Osaka beyond Universal — Osaka Castle, Dotonbori, street food
  • Add a day trip to Nara (from Kyoto) — the friendly deer park is perfect for kids
