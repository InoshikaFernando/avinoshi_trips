Universal Studios — Day 2

Beyond Harry Potter & Sushi in Osaka
🇯🇵 Japan· Osaka Osaka Fri 31 Jan 2025
With the Harry Potter area thoroughly explored on Day 1, our second day at Universal Studios Japan was about discovering the rest of the park. USJ has a fantastic range of areas beyond the Wizarding World — from Minion Park and Super Nintendo World to Jurassic Park and the Jaws ride. We wrapped up our Osaka stay with fresh sushi for dinner — because if there's one city in Japan where you absolutely must eat sushi, it's Osaka.
🎮 Beyond Harry Potter
Universal Studios Japan has grown enormously in recent years. Super Nintendo World is a visual spectacle — the Mario Kart ride uses AR headsets and is a must-do. Minion Park is perfect for younger kids, with the Despicable Me ride and plenty of character meet-and-greets. The Jaws ride is a classic water-based attraction. The park also has seasonal events and parades — in winter, the evening illuminations are spectacular.
🍣 Food Highlight — Sushi in Osaka
Sushi
Osaka is known as "Japan's Kitchen" (tenka no daidokoro) for good reason. The sushi here is fresh, affordable, and exceptional. Whether you go to a conveyor belt spot (kaiten-zushi) where kids can grab plates as they pass, or a sit-down restaurant, you're in for a treat. Don't be afraid to try things you wouldn't at home — the quality is a world apart.
💡 Guide Tips — USJ Day 2 & Osaka Food
  • Super Nintendo World: Entry often requires a timed ticket obtained from the app. Register first thing in the morning
  • Minion Park: Great for toddlers and younger kids — lower height requirements and less intense rides
  • Winter bonus: January means fewer crowds than spring/summer, and the park's winter illuminations are beautiful after dark
  • Osaka sushi tip: Kaiten-zushi (conveyor belt) restaurants are brilliant with kids — they can see what they're getting, prices are per plate, and there's no pressure to order quickly
  • Dotonbori area: If you're near Dotonbori in the evening, the neon signs and street food stalls are an attraction in themselves — takoyaki (octopus balls) are Osaka's signature street food
