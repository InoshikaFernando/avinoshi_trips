Japan Winter Adventure

Tokyo · Osaka · Kyoto
28 January — 7 February 2025 · 11 Days
A family travel guide for Japan in winter — 2 adults and 3 kids (ages 10, 7, and 3) exploring Tokyo, Osaka, and Kyoto over 11 days. Theme parks, bullet trains, ancient temples by rickshaw, immersive digital art, and some of the best food we've ever eaten. All with just 5 hand luggage bags — no checked baggage. We packed 3 day kits and 2 night kits, and washed clothes at Japan's excellent coin laundries every few days. Travelling via Fiji Airways from New Zealand — the cheapest route, with a 10+ hour Fiji layover that let us explore Nadi and cover two countries in one trip. Everything we did, what worked, what we'd change, and honest tips for families planning the same trip.
