Auckland to Fiji

The adventure begins
🇳🇿 New Zealand→🇫🇯 Fiji Transit Tue 28 Jan 2025
When you're a family of 5 who loves travelling, finding affordable flights is half the battle. Fiji Airways via Nadi is one of the cheapest routes from New Zealand to Japan, and that matters hugely when you're buying 5 tickets. The Fiji transit also means you can explore Nadi on the layover — we love covering as many countries as possible on every trip. We saved the Fiji exploration for the return leg (Day 11), when the 10+ hour layover gave us time to step out and enjoy a few tropical hours before flying home.
✈ Fiji Airways — Auckland to Tokyo
Route: Auckland (AKL) → Nadi (NAN) → Narita (NRT)
Depart: 14:05 AKL · Arrive 06:10 NRT (+1 day)
Duration: 20h 5min (via Nadi)
Cost: NZD $5,695 round trip for all 5 passengers (incl. taxes)
Baggage: 35kg carry-on + 5 checked bags included each way
Tip: Book seats together early — Fiji Airways lets you select seats at booking. Request a bassinet row if you have an infant
⚑ Highlights
  • Family of 5 sets off — 2 adults + 3 kids (ages 10, 7, and 3). Booking 5 tickets isn't easy on the wallet, but we love travelling and make it work
  • Fiji Airways — the most affordable NZ-to-Japan option. Comfortable, family-friendly, and the Fiji transit breaks the journey for little ones. The in-flight meals were a genuine surprise — really tasty for such an affordable airline
  • Hand luggage only — 5 carry-on bags, 100ml toiletry bottles from Kmart, and coin laundry every few days. No checked bags, no extra fees
  • Pack plenty of snacks, a change of clothes for the toddler, and tablets loaded with shows
🎂 Birthday Surprise on the Flight
January 29th is my birthday — and my husband and kids had a surprise planned. During our Fiji airport transit, they'd secretly bought a pendant for me from one of the duty-free shops. Then, on the overnight flight from Nadi to Tokyo — just as the clock ticked over to the 29th — they pulled it out and surprised me mid-air. The Fiji Airways air hostesses joined in and sang happy birthday. It was the sweetest possible start to our arrival in Japan, and one of those travel moments you never forget.

🧺 Coin Laundry in Japan
Coin laundries (コインランドリー) are everywhere — hotels, convenience stores, residential areas. A wash-and-dry cycle costs ¥400–¥600, takes ~90 minutes. Never run out of clean clothes.
💡 Guide Tips — Flying & Packing
  • Why Fiji Airways: Cheapest NZ-to-Japan option for a family of 5. The 10+ hour Fiji layover lets you explore Nadi — covering two countries in one trip
  • Hand luggage only: 5 carry-on bags. Rotate 3 day kits + 2 night kits. Wash every 2–3 days at coin laundries
  • Packing cubes: One cube per kit, colour-coded so kids can find their own clothes
  • Coin laundry tip: Buy detergent at a convenience store (¥100–¥200) — some machines have built-in, others don't
  • Shinkansen bonus: Hand luggage fits in overhead racks. Hop on and off trains without hassle
  • Park & Ride: Much cheaper than airport parking. Our 5 bags fit in a Toyota Aqua boot
  • eSIMs from Klook: We bought Japan eSIMs through Klook before the trip — 1GB/day, data only, 9 days. Instant activation, no fiddling with physical SIM cards, and reliable data from the moment you land. Essential for Google Maps, translation apps, and booking on the go. We also bought our Shinkansen tickets, teamLab, Universal, and DisneySea tickets through Klook
  • Google Translate — download Japanese: Before you leave, download the Japanese language pack in Google Translate for offline use. You will need it. Osaka locals are incredibly kind and helpful but there's a big language barrier — most people don't speak English. Kyoto is better for English, and Tokyo people can manage, but even there translation helps hugely. The camera mode (point at signs/menus) is a lifesaver for reading restaurant menus, train signs, and shop labels
  • Time zone: Japan is UTC+9 vs NZ UTC+12 in summer. Lose 3–4 hours going west
