Prepare & Book

Everything we booked, what it cost, and what to set up before flying
🇳🇿 NZ→🇯🇵 Japan 28 Jan — 7 Feb 2025
This page is your pre-trip checklist — every booking we made, what we paid (all in NZD), and the things you need to sort before you leave New Zealand. We booked most activities through Klook, hotels through Expedia and Booking.com, and flights direct with Fiji Airways. Here's the complete picture for a family of 5 (2 adults + 3 kids aged 10, 7, and 3).

Outbound: Auckland (AKL) 14:05 → Nadi → Narita (NRT) 06:10+1 · 20h 5min
Return: Narita (NRT) 16:30 → Nadi → Auckland (AKL) 23:40+1 · 27h 10min
Passengers: 2 adults + 3 children
Baggage: 35kg carry-on + 5 checked bags included each way
Total: NZD $5,695 round trip (all 5 passengers)
💡 Flight Tips
  • Book direct on the airline's website: Always check the airline's own site first — it's usually the cheapest option. Fiji Airways isn't always the cheapest NZ-to-Japan route, but they run regular offers and sales. We caught one of those offers, which made it the best deal for a family of 5. Keep an eye out for promotions — sign up for airline newsletters and check regularly
  • Why Fiji Airways worked for us: The Fiji transit breaks a long journey for kids, and the 10+ hour return layover let us explore Nadi — two countries in one trip
  • Book seats together early — Fiji Airways lets you select seats at booking. Request a bassinet row if you have an infant
  • Transit in Nadi: On the way out, the Nadi stop is short. On the return, it's 10+ hours — enough time to exit the airport and explore

💡 Hotel Tips
  • Book early: Japanese hotels fill up fast, especially near Disney and Universal. We booked 2+ months in advance
  • Family rooms: Look for "Deluxe" or "Family" room types — standard Japanese hotel rooms are small. All three hotels fit our family of 5
  • Disney partner hotel: Hotel Emion is much cheaper than official Disney hotels but runs free shuttle buses to the parks
  • Under-6 tip: Many hotels let under-6 kids stay free, but meals and extra bedding may cost extra
  • Check-in times: Most Japanese hotels are 15:00 check-in. If you arrive earlier, leave luggage at the front desk and explore

✅ Pre-Trip Checklist
  • Passports: Check all 5 passports are valid for the duration of the trip. Japan requires a valid passport but no visa for NZ citizens (stays under 90 days)
  • eSIM: Buy and install a Japan eSIM from Klook before departure. 1GB/day data-only plan, instant activation — essential for Google Maps, translation, and booking on the go
  • Google Translate: Download the Japanese language pack for offline use. You will need it — especially in Osaka where there's a big language barrier. The camera mode is a lifesaver for reading menus, signs, and train info
  • Klook bookings: Book eSIM, Shinkansen tickets, teamLab, Universal, and DisneySea well in advance. Save all e-tickets to your phone
  • Hotels: Book early — especially Disney partner hotels and Universal-area stays. Family rooms fill up fast
  • Packing: 5 hand-luggage bags. Organise into 3 day kits + 2 night kits with packing cubes. 100ml toiletry bottles from Kmart. Plan to use coin laundries every 2–3 days
  • Coin lockers: Most major train stations have coin lockers in small, medium, and large sizes (¥400–¥800). On our first day we dropped all 5 bags in lockers at Tokyo Station, explored hands-free, then picked them up before the Shinkansen. A lifesaver on travel days
  • Cash & cards: Bring some Japanese Yen cash for small purchases, coin laundries, and vending machines. Most tourist areas accept cards but smaller shops and restaurants may be cash-only
  • Travel insurance: Essential for a family trip — cover medical, cancellation, and lost luggage for all 5 travellers
  • Tokyo Disney Resort app: Download before the trip. You'll need it for Premier Access (skip-the-line) and wait times on the day
  • Universal Studios Japan app: Download for real-time queue times and park maps
  • Warm layers: Japan in January/February is cold (0–8°C). Pack thermals, warm jackets, gloves, and beanies — especially for Kyoto
